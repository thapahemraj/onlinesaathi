Our journey

From relief to rights to a scalable platform

Each milestone keeps us closer to the people and communities we serve.

2015

A digital mobile office for migrants

Helping migrants access jobs, their rights and legal aid in cities.

2018

Labour Resource & Support Centre

Launched with CSR funding, helping 20,000+ migrants access schemes, entitlements, wages and legal aid.

COVID-19

Relief for 12,500+ families

Food, rations, transport, flight tickets, livelihoods and loans for migrant labourer families.

2021

Shubhlaxmi Multiservices incorporated

A for-profit social enterprise, incorporated on 16 July 2021.

2022

#WalkForMigrant

5,100 km through 77 districts in 10 states, with 100+ RTIs and petitions to district collectors and the PMO.

2023

Online Saathi platform launched

Technology plus trained local Saathis — the 3T model goes live.

Today

2,000+ Saathis and 90,000+ workers reached

Growing access to trusted jobs, schemes, banking and remittance support.

Walk for dignity

11,111 km on foot, listening to workers

A grassroots padyatra led by our founder, walking alongside workers where they live and work to understand what dignified access really means.

Trusted last-mile access to schemes, jobs, banking and remittance for India’s informal workforce.
