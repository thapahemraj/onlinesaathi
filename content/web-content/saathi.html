Who can apply

Ideal Saathis

  • Shop owners, kiosk and cyber-café operators
  • Community volunteers and SHG members
  • Youth with basic smartphone skills
  • Anyone trusted in their neighbourhood or worker community
Flexible Saathi plans — monthly, 6-monthly and yearly — are shared during onboarding.
Do I need a shop?

No. A shop helps, but many Saathis serve their community from home or in the field.

What do I need to start?

A smartphone, basic KYC documents and the willingness to help people. We provide training and the app.

How soon can I start earning?

As soon as you complete verification and training and your Saathi ID is activated.
